## 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm **Gelar Wirabuana**, a full-stack software engineer and SaaS architect based in Indonesia with over **10 years of experience in .NET** development.

I'm a passionate **.NET backend engineer** with a strong foundation in enterprise systems and modern web technologies like **TypeScript, RemixJS, Prisma, and React**. I focus on building scalable, maintainable solutions for real-world businesses.

### 🧪 Founder of gelar-labs

**gelar-labs** is my personal innovation hub where I experiment, build, and launch new ideas — from developer tools to business platforms. It's where passion projects evolve into production-grade products.

### 💼 Current Projects

- **CureFlow** – SaaS platform for clinic and patient management  
- **Invieeu** – Event registration and ticketing system  
- **Hades-ERP** – Lightweight ERP tailored for SMBs  
- **Teskid** – AI-powered test bank and assessment tool

### 🌍 Vision

I believe software should **simplify lives, empower people**, and be accessible — especially in underserved markets. My goal is to create **open, practical, and impactful tools** that help developers and businesses scale efficiently.
